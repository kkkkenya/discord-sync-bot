## 2. Pinned how-to (#daily-logs)

**How to log**
• `/log` once a day keeps your streak. 15 minutes counts.
• Forgot yesterday? `/log` with `day: yesterday`. Made a mistake? `/unlog`.
• Your own course or interest: type its name in the track box, or `/interest add`.
• `/milestone done` the day you hit one. `/milestone own` for your own. `/milestone list` shows them all.
• Proof links are optional, but certificates, repos and videos help others learn from you.
• Be kind in the replies. Streaks break. Start again the next day.
