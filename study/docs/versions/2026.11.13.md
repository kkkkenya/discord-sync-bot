# Crux and the bot, 13 Nov 2026

## Done

- **Acrux goes on sale: KES 999 a semester.** The top plan has everything in Mimosa, plus Crux with 80 AI actions a day and every hosted model in the desktop app at any effort. Mimosa members upgrade for KES 499, which lasts until their Mimosa semester ends (a full semester when their Mimosa never ends).
- **Discord role "Acrux"** (`ROLES.vvip`). Acrux members hold Acrux, Mimosa and Gacrux together, so every channel and check that opens for Mimosa opens for them too.
- **Payments.** The form takes Gacrux, Mimosa, Acrux or Upgrade. "Upgrade" means the next plan up from what the member holds; "Acrux upgrade" also works. Claims for the Acrux upgrade from someone without Mimosa are flagged for staff. Receipts say what Acrux adds.
- **Renewals.** The daily job reminds a week before Acrux ends and takes the Acrux role away when it does. Mimosa stays when it runs longer.
- **Crux.** Tier `vvip` (shown as Acrux): 80 AI actions and unlimited downloads a day, DeepSeek for the deeper tasks, every relay model.
- **Dashboards** count Acrux members and Acrux payments. Announcements can target the Acrux role.
- **Database.** `supabase/crux.sql` widens the `crux_users.tier` check to allow `vvip`. Until it runs, an Acrux sign-in saves its row as `premium` and still gets Acrux from the session.
