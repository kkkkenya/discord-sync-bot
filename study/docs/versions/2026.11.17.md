# Crux web: 17 November 2026

## Done

- **Schemes, on every unit.** A new tab between Papers and Notes lists the unit's marking schemes from the hub, each beside the paper it marks when Crux can tell. One click opens the scheme in Crux's viewer. Papers with no scheme yet have "Ask for the scheme", which sends the owner a DM (once per student, paper and day). `server/schemes.js`, `GET /api/schemes`, `POST /api/schemes/ask`, `web/src/UnitSchemes.jsx`.
- **Marking schemes never use a download.** Students open them to check their own work, so `server/downloads.js` does not count a file named as a marking scheme and under 15 MB (a memo, or a larger file, still counts).
- **Practise opens on a due review first.** When Today has a review, Practise opens on that review's question, not the usual next question. Answers given from a review count toward the day's two reviews.
- **No access yet?** The sign-in screen has a section for people without a Discord account or without access: are you a member, have you paid for a plan, has an admin checked your account. Three steps, the #plans picture (`web/public/plans-channel.png`, cropped so no staff channels show), and buttons for Join the Study Hub and Open #plans. Someone signed in without a plan sees it open.
- **#plans, shorter.** The bot's price list is two cards: the plans, and how to pay, with the I've paid button and three reactions. Source `pc/announce/plans-channel.json`, pushed with `node ops/update-plans-message.js`.
- **EMM 302 CAT 1 2026/27 marking scheme** with free body diagrams, checked in Python, posted to the hub as a PDF. ECU 300 CAT 1 marked answers posted too.
- **Reviews in Today.** A topic that is green but fades within two days comes back as one question, shown as "Keep <topic>" after prepare and audit, before practise. At most two a day, and only the soonest paper's unit in its last three days. Answers from a review count toward the day's two. `server/review.js`, `server/review-store.js`, `server/next.js`, the Discord DM's today text.
- **What Crux knows about you.** In Settings, the lines Crux uses to fit explanations to you, each with its evidence: mistakes that recur (3 or more of one kind in 60 days), the step that holds you back across topics, ratings that run above or below your results, topics that keep breaking. Hide any line, add up to 5 notes of your own, or clear it all. Explanations, follow-up answers, the coach and checkpoint feedback use it; shared solutions and guides never do. Included in Export. `server/learner.js`, `server/learner-store.js`, `web/src/AboutYou.jsx`.
- **Anki export.** Cards from a unit's method recipes (what the method is, when to use it) and from questions you got wrong (the final answer and the step that broke). "Export cards to Anki" is on the unit's Sheet tab. Importing again updates the same cards. `server/anki.js` (ported from DeepTutor, Apache-2.0), `GET /api/export/anki?unit=`.
- **Retrieval eval, metrics only.** `tools/retrieval-eval.js` scores the current search path on the cases in `test/fixtures/retrieval/`: recall@5, recall@10, MRR. With no cases it says so. The metrics are ported from DeepTutor (Apache-2.0) in `tools/retrieval-metrics.js`. Retrieval is unchanged.
- **Study rooms.** Focus has two tabs: On my own and Rooms. Anyone can host a room: it gets a four-letter code and an invite link (`#/room/KXQT`). The host runs one clock for everyone: start, pause, skip, and Room settings for focus, breaks, long breaks, rounds, and whether the room waits for the host between blocks. The host can lock the room, make someone else host, remove someone, or end it. People in a hosted room see each other's names; public rooms show a count only. A second public room, the Long room, focuses from :00 to :50. Rooms end 30 minutes after everyone leaves, or after 12 hours. The rooms API reads only its own tables, so a full room polling every 20 s does not load anyone's study data. `web/src/room-clock.js`, `web/src/room-store.js`, `web/src/Rooms.jsx`, `server/rooms.js`, `server/rooms-store.js`, `server/rooms-routes.js`.

- **Code cells, for programming units.** Under each question in a programming unit's Practise (EMM 308 and any unit named for programming or coding): write Python, give it input, and run it on this device. Errors show the line in your own code. A program that runs for more than 3 seconds is stopped. Check with the sample compares your output with the question's sample output, line by line; once the worked solution is open, Compare with the model answer runs both with the same input. Plain Python only (no numpy or matplotlib). The interpreter (MicroPython, about 200 KB) loads the first time you press Run. No AI, nothing sent anywhere. `web/src/CodeCell.jsx`, `web/src/py-run.js`, `web/src/py-worker.js`, `web/src/code-check.js`.

- **Rooms for a class: today's work and check-ins.** The host sets today's work: a short line ("CAT 1 2024, questions 3 and 4") and, if they like, a Hub paper at a page. Everyone sees it under the clock and opens the paper in their own viewer. At each break, everyone answers "How did that block go?": Done, Partly or Stuck. The host sees the counts for the round, never who said what. Needs the second rooms SQL. `server/rooms.js` (cleanWork, checkinCounts), `POST /api/rooms/checkin`, `web/src/Rooms.jsx`.

- **Ask about a passage.** Select text anywhere in Crux (a page of a book or paper in the viewer, a question, a worked solution, a guide) and tap Ask beside Pin to notes. A sheet quotes the passage, offers three starters (Explain this in simpler words, Why is this true?, Show a small example) and keeps the thread while the page is open. For a Hub file, the tutor also reads the text around the passage. Each question is one AI action; Ask is hidden offline or without the AI. `POST /api/ask/passage`, `web/src/AskPassage.jsx`.

- **The done bar.** When a task ends (a right answer, a wrong one once its broken step is marked, a teach-back scored), a bar says what changed ("Torsion: amber → green", or "Saved") and offers Next, Today's first action that isn't the one just done, and Today. The check, photo, broke-here and teach-back replies carry the topic's status before and after, so it needs no extra request. `web/src/DoneBar.jsx`, `withChange` in `server/mastery.js`.
- **Answers left today.** A small line in Practise and in Ask ("12 answers left today"), from your day's AI count; it drops by one after each AI request and turns to the warning colour at 3. None for the owner.
- **When the AI answers run out,** Crux says what still works without them: practise solved questions, Learn, the sheet, or take a pack to any AI.
- **Ready, on Units.** A unit with a topic list and its files read shows Ready.
